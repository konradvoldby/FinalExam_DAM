## Final Project

**Title:** *The Banana Industry of Latin America *

**Research question:** 
what effects TNC’s have had on the Banana industry in the countries Honduras, Guatemala and Costa Rica, how changes to the banana industry have affected the economic growth of these countries and whether the industry have had a positive effect on the development of these countries.  

**Summary:** 
This project analyses the role of TNC's in Latin America. using historical literature and data visualizations. The data analysis is conducted in R using tidyverse, scales and patchwork, and processes data from The Our World in Data, the World Bank and the United Nations FAOSTAT databases.

**Key findings:**
The totality of our findings shows that the presence of transnational corporations in Latin America does not positively influence economic growth or benefit social factors when implemented in the form of enclave economies. The growth of industry can benefit a country if the industry is more integrated, but one big industry can leave countries less stable and resilient.
